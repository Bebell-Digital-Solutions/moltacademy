Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We aim to meet widely accepted international privacy standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

GDPR Ready CCPA Compliant

1. Information Collected

We may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address

Usage Data

  • Pages visited
  • Features used
  • Device type
  • IP address
  • Interaction data

Tracking Technologies

  • Cookies
  • Similar tracking technologies

Payment Information

  • Processed by PayPal
  • We do not directly collect or store full credit card details.

2. How Information Is Used

We use your information to:

  • Provide access to our platform and services
  • Communicate updates, service notifications, and important information
  • Improve our platform, features, and user experience
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries

We process data only for legitimate business purposes or where required by law.

3. Payment Processing

All financial transactions are processed by PayPal.

  • PayPal processes and stores financial data in accordance with its own privacy policy.
  • We do not store full credit card numbers or sensitive payment credentials on our servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information.

However:

  • No system can guarantee absolute security.
  • You provide information at your own risk.

We regularly review our security practices to reduce risk.

5. International Transfers

Your information may be stored or processed in countries outside your country of residence.

By using our services, you understand and agree that your data may be transferred internationally, subject to applicable legal protections.

6. GDPR Compliance For European Union Users

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

Access

Access the personal data we hold about you

Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate data

Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. CCPA Compliance For California Users

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Confirm that we do not sell your personal data

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements

When information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it based on operational and legal requirements.

9. Cookies Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enable core platform functionality
  • Analyze usage and improve performance
  • Remember user preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit certain features.

For privacy-related requests, questions, or to exercise your legal rights, contact:

Email: privacy@moltacademy.com
Response Time: We will respond within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable laws.
